# VoltaHub maintenance

## Polish is the source of truth

The Polish website HTML at the repository root and the original Polish Google Docs are always the authoritative reference. Verify every translation against Polish, including prices, areas, availability, lease terms, contact details and privacy wording. Update the Polish master first, then maintain the corresponding English HTML, Google Docs and PDF exports together. Do not change commercial terms while translating.

The user explicitly wants separately maintained static HTML files per language. Keep English under `/en/`; do not introduce a content generator or a shared content database. CSS, JavaScript and visual assets may be shared. Keep language links on corresponding pages, with matching anchors. Add only languages that are actually available.

Polish letting descriptions: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-mQ5-KfvI9sBqjEPSwHTBFvMIdtvk8ExkuQRTC8CxoA/edit
English letting descriptions: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uCWY9INj_3QQ_XnAqNgcPtGVe1hlGILEomPtIrXi8h0/edit

English PDFs in `en/brochures` must be exported from the corresponding editable English Google Docs, preserving the photographs and floor plans from the Polish sources. Check page fit after text changes. The Polish source takes precedence over English in every content review.
