About us

Alessandro Volta

Italian physicist, inventor, engineer and Franciscan tertiary. VoltaHub’s namesake. In 1800 he built the first electric battery. The unit of electric potential, the volt, is named after him. [Learn more]

A family business

We are a family business and own the building at Jutrzenki 94. We have been letting offices and warehouses here for over 20 years. We value long-term relationships, clear terms and peace of mind.
