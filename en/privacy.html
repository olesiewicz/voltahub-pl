Privacy

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Volta Property sp. z o.o., ul. Jutrzenki 94, 02-230 Warsaw, KRS 0000639404. For matters concerning personal data, you can email wynajem@voltahub.pl or write to the company’s postal address.

Letting enquiries

When you contact us about renting an office or warehouse, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, present an offer, arrange a viewing and discuss a lease. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, company name and the content of your message.

If you are considering entering into a lease in your own name, the legal basis for processing is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you contact us on behalf of a company or enquire for another purpose, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in handling your enquiry and discussing a lease (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). On the same basis, we may retain data where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Providing data is voluntary. To reply, we need contact details and enough information to understand your enquiry.

Email and retention periods

Messages sent to wynajem@voltahub.pl are forwarded through home.pl to the Gmail accounts used to handle enquiries. We store correspondence in Gmail. The home.pl forwarding service does not retain copies of messages.

If an enquiry does not result in a lease, we retain the correspondence for 6 months from the last message on the matter. We retain it for longer where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If we enter into a lease, we retain the necessary correspondence for its duration and then for the period required by law or necessary in connection with legal claims.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. During a visit, GitHub records your IP address for security purposes. Details of data processing and transfers outside the European Economic Area can be found in the GitHub privacy statement.

Google Maps and cookie settings

The embedded directions map loads only after you select “Accept” in the cookie banner. Consent covers displaying the map (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Once the map loads, your browser connects to Google, which receives information including your IP address and may use cookies or similar technologies. More information is available in the Google privacy policy and the description of Google cookies.

We remember your choice for 180 days using your browser’s local storage. You can change it at any time through “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page. Selecting “Reject” stops the embedded map from being displayed. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. You can still check directions using the standard “Open in Google Maps” link.

We do not use Google Analytics on the VoltaHub website.

Transfers outside the EEA

As we use Gmail, Google Maps and GitHub Pages, data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Google and GitHub describe their data transfer mechanisms, including participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for transfers covered by it. Information on mechanisms and safeguards is available in Google’s information on data transfers and in the GitHub privacy statement. You can also contact us about this.

Your rights

You have the right to access and rectify your data and, in the circumstances set out in the GDPR, to erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Where processing is based on our legitimate interest, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office.

We do not use enquiry data to make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
